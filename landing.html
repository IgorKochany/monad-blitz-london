How bidding works

Three steps. No dispatcher deciding your price.

Every ride follows the same path, and all three steps happen on a contract with no owner key and no admin switch.

1

You set the ceiling

Pick your pickup, your drop-off, and the most you'll pay. Choose Speed and it's escrowed now, or Save and it stays in your wallet until you accept someone.

Takes about 20 seconds
2

Drivers bid under it

Your ride lands in every nearby driver's feed instantly. They undercut each other to win it. You watch the price fall in real time.

First bids arrive in seconds
3

You confirm, they're paid

Escrow releases the moment you confirm drop-off. Whatever you didn't spend under your ceiling returns to your wallet in the same transaction.

Settles in 0.6 seconds
For drivers

Name your own price. Keep all of it.

You already know what a job across town is worth. Here you say the number instead of accepting one, and nothing is deducted afterwards.

  • See the fare before you commit. Distance, time and money up front, every time.
  • No account, no onboarding queue. One tap makes a wallet in your browser.
