Two ways to price a ride

Choose speed, or choose to save.

When you're in a hurry

Speed

Escrow your ceiling up front. The first driver to bid at or under it is matched instantly, and you're refunded the difference in the same block.

escrow ceiling → first bid ≤ ceiling wins → auto-refund the gap
When you want the best price

Save

Post with no money down and let drivers undercut each other. You watch the bids fall, then pick the one you want. Escrow only leaves your wallet when you accept.

post free → drivers undercut → you accept the bid you like
